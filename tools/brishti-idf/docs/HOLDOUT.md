# Hold-out validation of daily-to-sub-daily methods

Generated by `node tools/holdout.js` on 2026-10-27. Raw numbers: `docs/holdout.json`.

**Design.** Sub-daily data are hidden; only fixed calendar-day totals are used to estimate 24-h quantiles (with the 1.13 fixed-interval factor), which are then disaggregated. Errors are relative to quantiles fitted to the sliding-window annual maxima of the hidden sub-daily record (same distribution and estimator). "second half" rows calibrate any local exponent on the first half of the years and test on the second half (a realistic scenario: a short overlapping sub-daily record). "power-oracle" and "ratio-oracle" calibrate and test on the same years — optimistic bounds, not fair tests. "ratio-*" transfers the depth ratios P_t/P_24 (keeps the curvature of the depth-duration curve); "power-*" and "scaling-*" use a single exponent.

**Important limitations.** Denver is a single semi-arid, convective, mid-latitude station with July-only data — it is NOT representative of Bangladesh monsoon rainfall. The second dataset is synthetic. Neither establishes accuracy for Bangladesh; that needs BMD/BWDB sub-daily records.
