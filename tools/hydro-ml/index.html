All tools

Hydro ML Studio — train and compare machine-learning models to simulate or forecast water level, discharge or rainfall. Lag features, eight models (SVR, random forest, gradient boosting, neural network and more), cross-validation, automatic tuning and hydrology metrics.

🔒 Runs in your browser · files are never uploaded

Load a time series to begin

An .xlsx, .csv or .txt file with a date column and one or more numeric columns — for example daily rainfall, upstream water level and the downstream water level you want to predict. Dates and numbers are detected automatically.

Drop a file anywhere on this page, or press Ctrl+V to paste rows copied from Excel.
