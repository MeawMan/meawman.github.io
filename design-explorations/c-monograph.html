About

I am a civil and environmental engineer working as a Research Consultant at the Center for Environmental and Geographic Information Services (CEGIS), a public trust under the Ministry of Water Resources, Bangladesh, while pursuing an M.Sc. in Water Resource Development at IWFM, BUET. I build river models, read the Earth from orbit, and use machine learning to understand floods and droughts in a changing climate.

Research areas: hydroclimatology, drought and flood analysis, CMIP6 downscaling, remote sensing and GIS, river morphology, and hydrological tools. Current project: Feasibility Study for Integrated Water Resources Management in the Sandhya-Baleshwar River Basin.

Hydrodynamic modeling.
1D & 2D hydrologic and hydrodynamic models (SOBEK, Delft3D, SWMM) for river basin planning and urban drainage.
GIS and remote sensing.
Flood inundation mapping, LULC classification, river planform and erosion–accretion analysis with ArcGIS Pro & Google Earth Engine.
Climate projection.
CMIP6 statistical downscaling, bias correction (NBC, MBC, QBC), trend analysis and copula-based drought assessment.
Machine and deep learning.
RF, SVM, KNN, LSTM and CNN models for flood susceptibility, precipitation and water-level prediction.
